How it works

Many forms ask for a photo of 50 KB or less. Choose your image, keep 50 KB selected and press Resize. The preview shows the final size before you download.

If your form also needs exact pixel dimensions, fill in the width and height boxes. Leave them empty to keep the original proportions.

Always follow the exact size and format written in your official notification. Limits differ between exams and years.

Common questions

How do I get a photo under 50KB?

Upload the image, keep 50 KB as the maximum and press Resize. The tool lowers quality and size just enough to fit.

Why does the result look softer?

Fitting a large image into a small file means some detail is lost. Use the largest KB your form allows for the best look.

Is my image stored?

No. It never leaves your browser.
