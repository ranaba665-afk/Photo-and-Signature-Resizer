How it works

Railway recruitment forms check the size of every upload and reject files outside the allowed range. Set your limit below and download a ready JPG.

Scan or photograph your signature on plain white paper. Switch to the Signature tab, choose a small size, and check the preview before you download.

Always follow the exact size and format written in your official notification. Limits differ between exams and years.

Common questions

Where do I find the Railway photo size limit?

It is printed in the official recruitment notification, usually under the online application instructions.

Will resizing blur my photo?

The tool keeps quality as high as possible while staying under your KB limit. Very small limits will reduce sharpness.

Does it work offline?

After the page loads once, the resizing itself runs on your device, but keep a connection for the first load.
