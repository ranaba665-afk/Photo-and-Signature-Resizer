How it works

SSC application forms ask for a recent photo and a signature, each within a size range given in the notice. Read that range first, then set it here.

Use the Photo tab for your passport-style picture and the Signature tab for your signed scan. Sign on white paper with a dark pen and crop close for the clearest result.

Always follow the exact size and format written in your official notification. Limits differ between exams and years.

Common questions

How do I know the right size for my SSC form?

Open the official notification for your exam. The photo and signature limits are listed in the application section.

My file is still too big. What now?

Lower the KB value or enter a smaller width and height, then resize again.

Can I use a phone photo?

Yes. A well-lit photo with a plain background works. The tool shrinks large phone images easily.
